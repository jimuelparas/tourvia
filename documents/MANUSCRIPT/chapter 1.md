# Introduction

## 1.1 Project Context

In the current modern world, technology has become an important part of our daily life, impacting different fields such as communication, transportation, and tourism. The advancement of mobile technology has transformed the way in which one accesses information and the way by which things are being handled. Mobile applications have become more popular in the tourism sector to facilitate the process of organization, communication, and navigation to ensure the enrichment of the entire travel process. According to Yap *et al.* [1], smart tourism technologies have been closely linked to improved tourist satisfaction, underscoring the role of technology in enriching the travel process.

In the Philippines, tourism activities commonly involve guided tours, where tour guides are responsible for managing tourist groups, ensuring safety, and providing accurate information about destinations. However, most of the tour guides in the Philippines still rely on manual methods for checking attendance, managing the itinerary, coordinating the group, and lock of live tracking during the tour. These practices can lead to challenges in effectively tracking tourists, especially when members of a group become separated, and can complicate communication, particularly during fast-paced or large-scale tours. Research by Giao [2] shows that tourists increasingly rely on technology for both guidance and safety during their trips, yet such needs are still underdeveloped in many tour management systems.

In order to overcome these issues, this study proposes the development of a Tourism Management and Assistance Mobile Application that is accessible to the tourists under the supervision of a tour guide. The tour guide will be required to provide access to the application to the tourists, as well as making sure that the applications are accessible whenever the tourists are in an ongoing tour The application aims to support tour guides in managing tours more efficiently while providing tourists with guided information, communication, and safety assistance throughout the tour. As noted by Zhang [3], the adoption of mobile technologies in tourism significantly improves the coordination and safety of both tourists and tour guides, creating a more efficient and secure tour experience. In this mobile-based system, the tour guide is responsible for providing access to the application, ensuring that tourists can access critical information, updates, and emergency assistance during their tours.
