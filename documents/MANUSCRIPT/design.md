__4\.3 Design__

__	4\.3\.1 Output and User\-interface design__

__		__TourVia prioritizes simplicity, clarity, and user\-friendliness by providing a 	modern mobile interface that is easy to navigate for both tour guides and tourists\. The 	design focuses on delivering a smooth and organized experience, especially during real\-		time activities such as tracking, communication, and itinerary viewing\. The application 		uses a color scheme of light blue and white, where light blue represents trust, safety, and 	reliability, while white provides a clean and minimal background that enhances 	readability and reduces visual clutter\. The interface follows a consistent layout across all 	screens, with clearly designed buttons, cards, and navigation elements to improve 	usability\. Light blue is applied to primary buttons, headers, and active elements, while 	white is used for backgrounds and content areas\. Neutral colors such as black and gray 	are used for text and icons to maintain clarity and balance\. Overall, the TourVia interface 	is designed to be clean, accessible, and visually consistent, ensuring a comfortable and 	efficient user experience\.
